FAQ

What format are the guides in?

Printable US Letter PDFs you can read on screen or print and fill in by hand, with worksheets, trackers, and templates built in.

How do downloads work?

After checkout you receive a link to download your PDF instantly. No shipping, no waiting.

How does the clothing work?

Pieces are made to order, so nothing sits in a warehouse. Production and shipping timing are shown at checkout. Want something custom? Use the request button above.

Are the guides legal or medical advice?

No. They are general education and self-care support. If you’re in crisis, call or text 988 (U.S.).

Refunds?

Digital downloads are final sale. If something is wrong with your file, email me and I’ll make it right.

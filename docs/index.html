The story

Hileenlove is a little corner for the working human.

Hileenlove started with a simple idea: useful support should not sound like a corporate training video. The shop brings together honest tools for restaurant and hospitality workers, plus everyday goods that carry a little more meaning.

Everything is made independently by Hileen, with practical details, a warm point of view, and room for you to make it your own.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What format are the guides in?

Every guide is a printable, US Letter-sized PDF you can read on screen or print and fill in by hand. Each one includes worksheets, trackers, or templates built right in.

How do digital downloads work?

After checkout, you'll receive a link to download your PDF instantly. No shipping, no waiting.

How do print-on-demand goods work?

Goods are made to order, so nothing is produced just to sit in a warehouse. Production and shipping timing will be shown at checkout.

Are these guides legal or medical advice?

No. The guides on documenting mistreatment and workplace treatment are general education, not legal advice. The burnout guide is for self-care support, not medical treatment — if you're in crisis, call or text 988 (U.S.).

What's your refund policy?

Digital downloads are final sale. If something is wrong with your file, email us and we'll make it right. Unworn made-to-order goods can be reviewed case by case.
